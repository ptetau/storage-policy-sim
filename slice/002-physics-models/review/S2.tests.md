# Review of S2 (a tank in litres and degrees): tests

- minor: [tests] test_002_S2_R6_full_tank_loss_one_step A version that charges the loss of a full tank at every hot share would pass, because only a full tank is tested. D8 says the loss is for the hot share. Add a case with a half-full tank, where the loss is half of that of a full tank (no use, no request, cut-in share 0).
- minor: [tests] test_002_S2_R7_T2_guard_heats_at_step_80 A version that fixes the guard hour at 20 and ignores the guard hour of the tank would pass, because each guard test uses a tank with a guard hour of 20. Add one pair of tests with a tank of another guard hour, for example 18: the backstop heats at step 72 and not at step 71.
