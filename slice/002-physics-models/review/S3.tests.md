# Review S3: tests

- major: [tests] test_002_S3_R8_inverter_limit_caps_output_energy The test sums direct energy, `dischargeKwh` and export energy and asserts at most a quarter of the limit. Spec 001 R20 caps the energy to the house and the grid, so the cap applies to the net energy. `dischargeKwh` holds the energy out of the battery with the inverter loss added (D10: `0.02 kW + 0.015 × power`), so it is more than the energy that reaches the house. A correct version that serves exactly the limit to the house and loses a little more energy in the inverter passes the spec and fails this test. Fix: measure the house side. Use direct energy plus the covered load (load minus import) plus export, and assert that sum is at most a quarter of the limit. Keep the cases that need a cut (solar with large load, full battery with large load, solar surplus with a full battery) so that a version that ignores the limit still fails.
