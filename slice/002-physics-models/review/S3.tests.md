# Review S3: tests

- major: [tests] test_002_S3_R8_limits_hold_with_quarter_energy The test checks the export limit, the charge power and the discharge power, but never the inverter limit. A version that ignores the inverter limit of spec 001 (direct, battery and export energy together stay within a quarter of `inverterKw`) passes. Add a case with `inverterKw` above 0, for example 2.0, with a large load, solar and a full or part-full battery, and assert that direct plus discharge plus export is at most a quarter of the limit. The balance case with that limit only checks the balance, not the cap.
- minor: [tests] test_002_S3_R9_same_seed_and_day_same_cycles R9 says the cycles come from the seed and the day, but no test shows that the seed matters. A version that ignores the seed passes every R9 test. Add a check that two seeds give a different set of cycles on at least one day of the first 60 days.
