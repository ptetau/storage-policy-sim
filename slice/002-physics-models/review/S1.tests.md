# Review S1 (tests)

- minor: [tests] test_002_S1_R2_step_to_step_correlation A version whose clearness is constant inside each day (no random factor per step, against D4) passes, because the day-to-day swing alone gives a correlation above 0.5. Add a check that the steps of a day are not all equal, for example that at least some days have two different step values, and that the steps of a day stay near the day value.
- minor: [tests] test_002_S1_R2_summer_mean_above_winter_mean A version of `clearness` that ignores `dayClearness` (for example one with no winter dip) passes, because both R2 season tests and the day correlation test read `dayClearness` only. Add one check on `clearness` itself: the mean of the steps of days 0 to 89 is above the mean of days 152 to 242.
- minor: [tests] test_002_S1_R3_clear_day_north_panel_beats_south_panel A version that gives the light on a tilted panel without dividing the direct light on flat ground by the sun height, or that drops the ground light, passes every R3 test, because the tilted panel tests compare only two panels or read a ratio. Add one magnitude check from D5, for example a clear sky and a north panel at 30° at day 0, step 48, within a few W/m² of a value that the test computes by hand from D5.
- minor: [tests] test_002_S1_R5_inlet_lags_the_air The check that the air peak is above 0 holds for any version and says nothing about the spec. Remove it.
