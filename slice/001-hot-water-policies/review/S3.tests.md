- major: [tests] test_001_S3_R7_thermostat_T1_below_cut_in_backstop_heats It looks for an hour that starts below the cut-in, and finds none, so it fails on a correct slice. In the tank model the thermostat decides inside the hour, on the level after the loss, the draw and the policy heating, and the backstop heats in that same hour. So a tank almost never starts an hour below the cut-in. Fix: in the run with 0 heating hours, take each hour whose level after the loss and the draw (the energy of the hour before, less 0.6% of it, less the draw of the hour) is below the cut-in, and require a backstop above 0 in that same hour. Require that at least one such hour exists.
- major: [tests] test_001_S3_R7_thermostat_T2_guard_hour_heats_tank_not_full It needs days that start from a full tank, and finds none, because a tank is full only in the hour in which the heating ends. Fix: with a cut-in of 0 and a floor of 0, take each day on which no hour before hour 20 ends with a full tank, and require a backstop above 0 at hour 20 of that day. Require that at least one such day exists.
- major: [tests] test_001_S3_R7_thermostat_T3_full_tank_stops_heating It needs days that reach a full tank in the evening by its own rule, and finds none. Fix: take each hour that ends with a full tank (within 0.000000001 kWh) while the level stays above the cut-in in the next hour, and require a backstop of 0 in that next hour, unless the next hour is a guard hour of a day that was not full yet. Require that at least one such hour exists.
