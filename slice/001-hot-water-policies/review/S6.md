# Review S6: code

- minor: [architecture] StoragePolicySim/Simulation.lean:230 `stepHourLimited` repeats the charge and discharge arithmetic of `stepRequested` (room to fill, power, loss, soc clamp), so a later change of the battery step needs two edits. The `0` limits shortcut at the top is also redundant, because the general path with `noCap` gives the same flows. Fix: pass the caps (direct, discharge room, export cap) into one shared step, or derive the limited flow from `stepRequested`.
- minor: [requirement] StoragePolicySim/PolicyCommands.lean:159 `--pv` typed after `--array` is dropped without a message, because any `--array` wins over `--pv` whatever the order. The interface says `--pv` replaces the arrays with one flat array. Fix: let the last of `--pv` and `--array` decide, with the first `--array` replacing the arrays of the home and each later `--array` adding one.
