- major: [tests] test_001_S2_R9_dynamic_heats_only_with_surplus_or_low_tank This test of the done slice S2 (in tests/slice/S2.lean) fails on a correct slice, because R9 changed with D26 (no thrashing): the dynamic policy can also ask for heat in an hour in which it keeps heating a tank that it asked to heat in the hour before. Fix: an hour with a request is allowed when its solar energy is more than its house load, or its tank held less than 5.25 kWh before the hour, or the tank had a request above 0 in the hour before (for hour 0 of a day, the hour before is hour 23 of the day before). An hour with a request and none of the three must still fail the test.
