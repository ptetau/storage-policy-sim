# Spec: hot-water policies

Status: draft

## Purpose

The `sim` command simulates a home with solar panels, a battery and an electric hot-water tank. The owner uses it to find the best daily profile for heating the hot water and charging the battery. The best profile uses the most of the home's own solar energy, never leaves the tank cold, and holds over many weather years. This spec adds static, precomputed and dynamic policies to the simulator that exists.

## Decisions

- D1 (base): The code that exists is the base, and its behaviour stays: the scenario (sun, weather, load), the battery strategies, the hourly simulation, the simulated Solcast forecast (with the shared error of a day and `forecastOf`), and the hot-water tank (`tankStep`, `drawKwh`, `drawFactor`). Only the usage line of `sim` changes.
- D2 (stack): Lean 4.35.0 with Lake. The new library modules are `StoragePolicySim/Policy.lean`, `StoragePolicySim/HomeSim.lean` and `StoragePolicySim/PolicyCommands.lean`. `stepHourGated` goes into `StoragePolicySim/Simulation.lean`.
- D3 (battery): The battery charges only from the solar surplus, and never from the grid, because the aim is self-consumption.
- D4 (policies): There are three kinds of policy, with the names `static`, `precomputed` and `dynamic`. A static policy repeats one profile each day. A precomputed policy plans each day from the forecast. A dynamic policy decides each hour from what it sees.
- D5 (default tank): 10.5 kWh, a 3.0 kW element, a loss of 0.6% of the stored energy each hour, a thermostat cut-in at 5.25 kWh, a floor of 2.1 kWh (below it the tank counts as cold), and a guard hour of 20. The tank module exists with these values.
- D16 (daily sterilising): The tank must be full at least once each day, to sterilise the water. The time of day does not matter, so a policy is free to fill the tank at any hour. If the tank has not been full by 20:00, the thermostat fills it from the grid. 20:00 leaves the 3.0 kW element enough hours before midnight. The rule is always on, and no option changes it.
- D17 (tuner goal): The tuner has two goals, and the person chooses one. `self-consumption` (the default) ranks by the highest mean self-consumption, and the lowest mean import breaks a near tie. `import` ranks by the lowest mean grid import, and the highest mean self-consumption breaks a near tie. A near tie is 0.05 points of self-consumption, or 0.5 kWh of import.
- D6 (default home): latitude -41.3, 5 kW of panels, 20 kWh of load each day with 3 kWh flexible, a battery of 10 kWh with 5 kW, the default tank, 6.0 kWh of hot water each day with a spread of 0.25 from day to day, 10 forecast fetches each day, and a forecast error scale of 1.0.
- D7 (default policies): static: heat from 10:00 for 4 hours with a 3.0 kW element, and discharge from 17:00. Precomputed: a target of 10.5 kWh (a full tank), a minimum forecast of 0.5 kWh for an hour to heat, and discharge from 17:00. Dynamic: a low level of 5.25 kWh, a target of 10.5 kWh, and discharge from 17:00.
- D8 (run): A run starts with a full tank and an empty battery, on 1 January. In each hour, the policy gives an action, then the tank runs its hour, then the heating and the backstop heating join the house load, then the battery runs its hour.
- D9 (planning): A precomputed policy plans at hour 0 of each day. It uses the forecast of the fetch at hour 0, the energy in the tank, and the expected draw of the day (the draw profile with the season, without the random factor of the day). A dynamic policy reads the latest fetch. The forecast uses the seed of the run.
- D10 (safe): A policy is safe over a set of seeds when no seed has an unmet draw. The `safe` column of `sim policy robust` also needs 0 cold hours.
- D11 (comparison rows): `thermostat` (the static policy with 0 heating hours), `static`, `precomputed`, `dynamic`, and `perfect-forecast` (the precomputed policy with a forecast error of 0), in this order.
- D12 (tuner grid): The tuner tries each static profile with a start from 6 to 16, from 1 to 8 heating hours, and discharge from 15 to 22. That is 704 candidates, in the order of the start, the heating hours and the discharge hour.
- D13 (output): An energy prints with one decimal and `kWh`, and a ratio with one decimal and `%`. A line of `sim policy run` is a label, spaces to column 19, and a value. A table has its header line, and its columns align by spaces.
- D14 (errors): A bad command line prints one line `error: <CODE>: <detail>` on stderr, prints nothing on stdout, and exits with 2. The codes are `UNKNOWN_COMMAND`, `UNKNOWN_OPTION`, `MISSING_VALUE`, `BAD_NUMBER`, `OUT_OF_RANGE`, `UNKNOWN_POLICY` and `UNKNOWN_GOAL`. A number is an optional sign, digits, and an optional point with digits. The last value of an option counts.
- D15 (option ranges): `--tank` 0 to 50, `--element` 0 to 20, `--cut-in` 0 to 50, `--floor` 0 to 50, `--draw` 0 to 100, `--draw-cv` 0 to 1, `--fetches` 1 to 10 (whole), `--forecast-error` 0 to 5, `--heat-start` 0 to 23 (whole), `--heat-hours` 0 to 24 (whole), `--discharge-from` 0 to 24 (whole), `--target` 0 to 50, `--min-solar` 0 to 10, `--low` 0 to 50, `--seeds` 1 to 100 (whole), `--hour` 0 to 24 × days - 1 (whole), `--lon` -180 to 180. The options of `sim run` keep their ranges.

## Requirements

- R1 (gated battery step): `stepHourGated battery socKwh allowCharge allowDischarge pvKwh loadKwh` gives the flows of one hour by the rules of `stepHour` with the `greedy` strategy. A battery that may not charge keeps the surplus as export. A battery that may not discharge leaves the shortfall as import. With both allowed, each flow equals `stepHour` with `greedy`. With discharge allowed only from 17:00, each flow equals `stepHour` with `evening`.
- R2 (static profile): `staticProfile params` gives 24 actions. The tank asks for the element power in each hour from `heatStart` for `heatHours` hours, and for 0 kWh in the other hours. The window stops at the end of the day. The battery may always charge. It may discharge from the hour `dischargeFrom` to the end of the day, and a value of 24 never lets it discharge.
- R3 (day-ahead plan): `planDay tank params forecastKwh tankKwh expectedDrawKwh` gives 24 actions. The need is the target less the tank energy plus the expected draw, and never less than 0. Only an hour with a forecast of at least `minSolarKwh` can heat. The plan gives the element power to these hours, from the highest forecast to the lowest, until the need is met. The last hour gets only the rest of the need. A tie goes to the earlier hour. A need that the hours cannot meet stays unplanned. The battery rules are those of R2.
- R4 (dynamic action): `dynamicAction tank params observation` gives the action of one hour. With a solar surplus and a tank below the target, the tank asks for the smallest of the element power, the surplus and the room to the target. If not, and the tank is below the low level, and the forecast for the rest of the day is less than the lack (the low level less the tank energy), the tank asks for the smaller of the element power and the lack. In every other case the tank asks for 0 kWh. The battery rules are those of R2.
- R5 (defaults and names): `defaultStatic`, `defaultPlan` and `defaultDynamic` hold the values of D7. `Policy.name` gives `static`, `precomputed` or `dynamic`.
- R6 (hourly home flows): `runHome setup policy seed days` gives one record for each hour, in order of day, then hour. Each record holds the solar energy and the house load of the scenario hour, the draw (the draw profile times the random factor of the day), the heating that the policy asked for, the result of the tank hour, the battery gate, and the battery flow of `stepHourGated` for the house load plus the heating plus the backstop heating. The run follows D8 and D9. The same arguments give the same run.
- R7 (no cold tank): For the default home and each default policy, no hour has an unmet draw, the tank never ends an hour below its floor, and the tank is full at least once each day. This holds with a draw spread of 0.25 and of 0.5. A static policy with 0 heating hours gets all its heat from the backstop. An element that is too weak for the draw gives an unmet draw and cold hours.
- R8 (totals and ratios): The totals of a run add up its hours: solar, load (the house load, the heating and the backstop heating), import, export, battery loss, tank loss, heating, backstop heating, draw, unmet draw, cold hours, the lowest and the final tank energy. The self-consumption is 100 × (solar - export) / solar. The self-sufficiency is 100 × (load - import) / load. A ratio with nothing to divide by is 0%. The energy of the solar, the battery and the tank balances. A run of 0 days has totals of 0, except the lowest and the final tank energy, which are the energy of the full tank.
- R9 (policies at work): In a run, a static policy asks for heat only in its window. A precomputed policy with a perfect forecast asks for heat only in hours whose solar energy is at least the minimum forecast. A dynamic policy asks for heat only in an hour with a solar surplus, or with a tank below the low level. The default static policy and the default dynamic policy each give a higher self-consumption than the static policy with 0 heating hours.
- R10 (robust evaluation): `evaluate setup policy seed seeds days` runs the policy for each seed from `seed` to `seed + seeds - 1`. It gives the mean self-consumption, the worst (lowest) self-consumption, the mean self-sufficiency, the mean import, export and backstop heating, the total unmet draw, the count of seeds with an unmet draw, and the sum of the cold hours. A count of 0 seeds gives 0 in each field.
- R11 (static tuner): `tuneStatic setup goal seed seeds days` evaluates each candidate of D12 with the element of the home tank. A candidate is safe by D10. The best is the safe candidate that ranks first by the goal of D17. A candidate within the near tie of the first has the same standing, and then the second measure of the goal wins. A tie goes to the first candidate. With no safe candidate, the best is the candidate with the lowest total unmet draw. The result holds the best profile, its evaluation, the count of candidates, and the count of safe candidates.
- R12 (usage line): `sim`, `sim help` and `sim --help` print the usage and exit with 0. The first line is `usage: sim run [options] | sim compare [options] | sim forecast [options] | sim policy <command> [options]`. `sim policy` and `sim policy help` print `usage: sim policy run|compare|robust|tune [options]` first. An unknown word after `sim policy` gives `error: UNKNOWN_COMMAND: policy <word>`.
- R13 (forecast command): `sim forecast` prints the Solcast JSON of one fetch and a line break, and exits with 0. Its options are `--hour` (default 0), `--lon` (default 174.8), `--forecast-error` (default 1), and `--lat`, `--pv`, `--days` and `--seed` of `sim run`. Any other option is an unknown option.
- R14 (policy run): `sim policy run` simulates one policy and prints 13 lines: `policy`, `days`, `seed`, `solar`, `load`, `import`, `export`, `heating`, `backstop`, `unmet-hot-water`, `cold-hours`, `self-consumption` and `self-sufficiency`. The values are the totals of `runHome`. `--policy` picks `static` (the default), `precomputed` or `dynamic`. Another name gives `error: UNKNOWN_POLICY: <name> (static, precomputed, dynamic)`. The options are those of `sim run` except `--strategy` and `--monthly`, and the home, tank and policy options of D15 except `--seeds`.
- R15 (policy compare): `sim policy compare` prints the header `policy self-consumption-% self-sufficiency-% import-kWh export-kWh backstop-kWh` and one row for each row of D11, on the same weather. It takes the options of `sim policy run` except `--policy`.
- R16 (policy robust): `sim policy robust` prints the header `policy mean-self-consumption-% worst-self-consumption-% mean-self-sufficiency-% mean-export-kWh mean-backstop-kWh unmet-kWh cold-hours safe` and one row for each row of D11, from `evaluate` over `--seeds` seeds (default 10) from `--seed`. `safe` is `yes` or `no`, by D10.
- R17 (policy tune): `sim policy tune` prints the lines `goal`, `candidates`, `safe`, `heat-start`, `heat-hours` and `discharge-from` of the best static profile of `tuneStatic`, a blank line, and the table of `sim policy robust` with that profile in the row `static`. `--seeds` has the default 5. `--goal` picks `self-consumption` (the default) or `import`, and another name gives `error: UNKNOWN_GOAL: <name> (self-consumption, import)`. The options `--heat-start`, `--heat-hours` and `--discharge-from` are unknown options here.
- R18 (option errors): Each option of D15 accepts the lowest and the highest value of its range. A value outside gives `error: OUT_OF_RANGE: <option> <value> (<lowest> to <highest>)`. A value that is not a number, or not whole where D15 says whole, gives `error: BAD_NUMBER: <option> <value>`. An option with no value gives `error: MISSING_VALUE: <option>`. An option that a command does not take gives `error: UNKNOWN_OPTION: <word>`.

## Out of scope

- Charging the battery from the grid, and tariffs or costs.
- Steps shorter than one hour, and a policy that plans again at each fetch (IN-0009 of the pdlc inbox).
- Guards on the inputs of the library functions (IN-0005 of the pdlc inbox).
- Calls to the real Solcast service.
